⭐ The Silent Streets

Why are the streets so silent and cold,

Maybe it's me, I might have turned old;

Those streets contained countless stories,

On its pavement we created our own memories;



The road looked empty, lights glowing dim,

Going farther I saw a boy, I called to him;

"What are you doing ?" I asked,

He smiled at me, though something remained masked;



He asked "can you take me to home ?",

He led the way, I followed to his home;

He looked so playful, running on the road,

He ran where once my own footsteps flowed;



He kept talking, I listened to what he told,

Suddenly he asked,"why do you think streets are so cold ?";

What do I tell him of the life he hadn't known,

The seed of reality still hadn't been sown;



His smile and energy lit up the way,

It looked as if it had turned into the day;

We reached his home our journey came to an end;

Seeing his innocence my heart started to mend;



He waved goodbye, and I walked back alone,

Yet somehow, the road felt more like home.





⭐ First Sight

I saw it in her eyes when she looked towards me,

I thought it must be a dream, I should let it be;

Suddenly, the moon turned brighter;

My heart was heavy, it started to feel lighter;

The world began so shift as I watched her go,

"What am I feeling ?"even I don't know.



His eyes felt deep, like an endless ocean that day,

She felt this feeling strange;

His personality glowed with the brightness of the sun;

She couldn't tilt her head, yet she wanted to turn;

I should have sat there, but I just couldn't,

"Talk to him" my heart said, but my mind wouldn't;

Maybe it's time I should probably leave,

I could have told him but would he believe ?;



They moved on and never met again,

The departure only grew to their pain.

⭐ The Alchemy Of Belonging

Late at night, I sat on the bench, wide awake,

With a heavy heart, staring deep into the lake;

I thought, *“This place will soon become my identity,

Was it really my choice, or was it just destiny?”



My room turned small, my dreams increased,

People often question, “Would you ever be pleased?”

I met new people, changed my town,

I want to become someone upon whom people don't frown.



The wind started to blow, the water started to ripple,

In an earshot, I started to hear a whistle;

It came from someone unknown, but still made me calm,

The coldness vanished, the warmth reached my palm.



The waves stopped; I saw the stars in the lake,

And for a second, I felt this was no mistake;

I didn't know this place could hold me awake,

Perhaps, this is a new home I could make.





⭐ The Longest Night

My mind raced, I had a feeling,

Had I done enough or was I just dreaming;

I thought it might just be a nightmare,

I looked into the mirror and saw a face that held despair;

My heart kept racing, my mind couldn't follow,

From the mirror my eyes felt hollow;

Even though I tried to keep my calm,

My mind ignored its own alarm;

The twilight held no stars to see,

The shackles were heavy, no hand could set them free;

The only way I could escape was to perish,

But i couldn't leave the things I used to cherish;

I told myself there was more left to fight,

And slowly I drifted away into the night.
